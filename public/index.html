Frequent Viva Voce Questions & Direct Answers

Q1: Why did you use Graph Neural Networks instead of XGBoost or Random Forest? ↓
Answer: Traditional models like XGBoost only analyze individual tabular features (amount, time). They cannot capture relational topology. In UPI fraud, money launderers pass funds across multiple accounts linked to the same phone (device_id). GNNs detect these multi-hop collusion patterns through neighborhood message passing.
Q2: Why GraphSAGE instead of standard Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN)? ↓
Answer: Standard GCN is transductive — it requires the full Laplacian matrix and must be retrained whenever a new transaction arrives. GraphSAGE is inductive — it trains neural aggregators that generalize to new, unseen streaming transactions on the fly without retraining.
Q3: How do you handle extreme class imbalance (fraud is <2%)? ↓
Answer: We replaced standard Cross-Entropy Loss with Focal Loss (α=0.85, γ=2.0). The modulating factor (1 - p_t)^γ down-weights easy clean transactions and concentrates gradient updates on rare, hard-to-detect fraudulent transactions.
Q4: Does this fit within NPCI's real-time UPI transaction latency SLA? ↓
Answer: Yes. NPCI UPI transactions require responses within 100 ms. Our PyTorch Geometric GraphSAGE pipeline executes in 18.4 ms (P99 < 35 ms) on commodity hardware, leaving ample headroom for bank core switches.
